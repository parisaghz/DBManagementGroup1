# 🎬 Emotional Dynamics & Box Office Success

This project explores how **emotional tones in user and expert movie reviews** influence a film’s box office performance. Through SQL, Python, and sentiment analysis, we investigate how audience perception—captured in the emotional tone of reviews—relates to financial success across genres.

## 🎯 Main Research Question

**How do emotional dynamics in user and expert reviews influence the overall box office performance of movies?**

We formulated 5 testable hypotheses, including:
- Emotional tone varies by genre
- Expert reviews matter most during opening weekends
- Production budgets reduce the impact of negative reviews
- Literary genres are more sensitive to negative sentiment
